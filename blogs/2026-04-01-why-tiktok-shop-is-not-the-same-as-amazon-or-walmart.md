What does this mean for brands? It means that simply listing your products and optimizing keywords (the way you would on Amazon/Walmart) isn’t enough on TikTok. In fact, treating TikTok Shop like just another e-commerce channel – focusing only on product listings and paid ads – “misses the point entirely.” Success on TikTok requires creating consumer intent through content, rather than capturing existing demand through search.
